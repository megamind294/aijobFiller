DATA USE · UPDATED 16 OCTOBER 2026

Your information in JobPilot.

JobPilot is an independent job-search application operated on this Azure installation. It is not Google, Microsoft, LinkedIn, an employer, or a recruiting agency.

Account and resume storage

JobPilot stores account details, password hashes, uploaded resumes, profile drafts, saved jobs, application documents and activity on its server. Customer workspaces are separated by account. The server operator administers this installation; this is not end-to-end encrypted storage. Records remain until removed by an implemented account action or by the operator, and backups may retain earlier copies.

Optional Gmail connection

Gmail access is optional and uses Google's own authorization page with read-only permission. Never enter your Google password in a JobPilot form. After you connect and choose Sync, candidate recruiting messages are fetched and stored for routing to your applications. Tokens are encrypted on the server. Disconnect removes the locally stored authorization; you can also revoke access in your Google Account. Existing imported messages and local drafts remain. JobPilot does not send email from this integration.

Optional AI requests

When you use the assistant, choose AI document preparation, or request a Gemini email draft, relevant text is sent to Google Gemini. This may include profile context, job descriptions, your question, or the selected email. These actions are optional. Review generated text before using it. JobPilot requests non-stored Gemini interactions; Google's applicable API policies still govern provider processing.

Payments and cookies

Payment details are entered on Stripe's hosted checkout, not in JobPilot. This installation currently uses Stripe test mode, with checkout limited to designated test accounts. No live customer payment collection is enabled. JobPilot uses a secure session cookie for sign-in and form protection. This page loads no advertising or third-party scripts.

Questions or security concerns

The source repository and public issue tracker are linked below. Do not post passwords, API keys, resumes or private messages in public issues. Until a private support channel is configured, do not upload information you cannot entrust to this installation's operator.

INDEPENDENT JOB-SEARCH WORKSPACE

Keep your next steps together.

JobPilot helps you review your resume, search selected public company job boards, prepare application documents and track your progress.

This is the JobPilot installation for the aijobFiller project, hosted on Azure. It is not affiliated with Google, Microsoft, LinkedIn or the employers whose public postings appear in search.

Create a JobPilot accountSign in to JobPilot

Know what you are signing in to

Use a password created specifically for JobPilot. Never enter another service's password here. Optional Google sign-in and Gmail connection happen on Google's own authorization pages.

Available workflows

  • Upload a PDF or DOCX resume, recover text and review a profile draft.
  • Search six configured public company job boards on demand.
  • Prepare and review documents; unsupported employer forms require manual completion.
  • Optionally connect Gmail read-only for on-demand routing and review local reply drafts.

JobPilot does not guarantee interviews, offers or application acceptance. Owner access is free. Customer workspaces require paid access; live payments are not yet enabled on this installation.
